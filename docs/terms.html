What SNAG is, and is not

SNAG does not host, store, index, stream or distribute any video. It holds no library and no catalogue. It takes a link you supply and fetches the file from that site, on your device, in much the same way your browser requests a page. Nothing passes through any server of mine, because there isn't one.
